### Q1: What does "Legal: 0 Escrow Stays" mean?

#### In Simple Terms:
* **Escrow**: A dedicated, interest-bearing bank account maintained under the jurisdiction of the District Principal Civil Court pursuant to **Section 3H(4) of the National Highways Act, 1956**.
* **Stay Order**: A judicial injunction issued by a court that temporarily halts the disbursement or release of compensation to claimants due to contested ownership or title succession disputes.
* **"0 Escrow Stays" means**:
  > **Currently, no court has issued an injunction freezing the compensation funds for this project.** The money deposited in the court account is legally clear to be disbursed as soon as the Competent Authority for Land Acquisition (CALA) finishes verifying the claimants' mutation and title records.

### Q2: What does "Statutory Legal Liquidation Horizon" mean?

#### In Simple Terms:
* **Plain English Term**: **"Time Needed to Settle All Land Court Disputes & Complete All Payouts."**
* **The Dual-Track Reality**:
  * **Physical Civil Track**: Under **Section 3D(2) of the NH Act 1956**, publication in the Official Gazette vests the land *absolutely in the Central Government free from all encumbrances*. Because of this, highway construction proceeds legally, and public vehicles drive at 120 km/h.
  * **Statutory Legal Track**: Even though the road is built and open to traffic, unresolved title disputes and enhanced compensation demands filed by landholders under **Section 3H(4)** continue to be litigated in the District Court.
* **Why it matters**:
  While traffic is moving, the government exchequer continues to accumulate **9% to 15% statutory penal interest** per year on unpaid or contested compensation until every reference case is formally resolved. The **Statutory Legal Liquidation Horizon** measures the time required to settle these cases, disburse the locked court funds, and extinguish this growing financial liability.

### Q3: What does "Total Case-Free Horizon: 1,349 Days (1,226d in Escrow + 123d AI Residual)" mean? Explain with an example.

#### 🚗 The Real-World Example:
Imagine you buy a house:
1. **The Physical Side**: You received the keys, moved in, painted the rooms, and your family has been **happily living in the house for 3 years**.
2. **The Legal Side**: Two brothers who previously co-owned the land are fighting in court over who gets the payout. The court ordered your payment locked in a **court bank account (Escrow)** until the judge resolves their succession dispute.

* **Is your house built?** Yes, you are living in it.
* **Is the legal file closed?** No, the court case is still pending.

#### 🧮 Breaking Down the Equation:

$$\mathbf{1,349\text{ Days Total}} = \mathbf{1,226\text{d in Escrow (Past)}} + \mathbf{123\text{d AI Residual (Future)}}$$

1. **1,226d in Escrow (Past)**:
   * The disputed compensation has already been sitting in the District Court's escrow account for **1,226 days (~3.3 years)** while hearings and title examinations were conducted.
2. **+123d AI Residual (Future)**:
   * Our Machine Learning model (trained on historical patterns of similar corridors) forecasts that it will take **another 123 days (~4 months)** for the Land Acquisition Officer and the District Judge to complete the final documentation and release the remaining payments.
3. **1,349 Days Total**:
   * The complete lifecycle duration—from the day the land dispute entered the court until the project becomes **100% case-free, with every rupee disbursed and all legal liabilities extinguished**.
